Meet Your Instructor

Kyle Geske is a programmer, teacher, artist, and father. 

Over his career Kyle has worked as a support technician, an engineer in the telecommunications industry, a contract programmer and consultant, an entrepreneur, and a college instructor. He is also the executive director of Open Democracy Manitoba, a non-profit organization that builds online tools to foster government accountability, transparency and public education.

Kyle’s technical knowledge spans the entire stack, from hardware and FPGA design, to firmware programming in assembly language, through driver and network coding, system and database administration, back and front end web development, mobile app development, data visualization, generative art, and most recently game development.

He is an open-source contributor and a champion of open government ideas and open data best-practices.

Kyle is passionate about coding, math, language, and philosophy. He's an avid reader, a podcast devotee, and a board game player. He has two young children who play way more video games than he does.
