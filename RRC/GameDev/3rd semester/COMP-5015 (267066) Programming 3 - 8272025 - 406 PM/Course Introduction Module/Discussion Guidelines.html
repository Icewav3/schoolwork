Course Code of Conduct 

Be Respectful

From RRC Policy S1 – Student Code of Rights and Responsibilities:

Students have the responsibility to contribute to maintaining a respectful and accepting learning environment which is free from fear, harm, discrimination, harassment and intimidation.  

Our Code of Conduct 

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe and welcoming environment for all, regardless of gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, nationality, or another similar characteristic. 

YES PLEASE - Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive vibe include: 

  • Being kind and courteous to others. 

  • Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences. 

  • Showing empathy towards our fellow classmates and instructors. 

  • Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes. 

NO THANK YOU - Examples of unacceptable behaviour in our classroom tools and spaces include: 

  • The posting or display of inappropriate images or language, including but not limited to sexualized or violent imagery or language. 

  • Discriminatory jokes and language. 

  • Spamming, flaming, baiting or other attention-stealing behavior. 

  • Trolling, insulting, derogatory comments, and personal attacks. 

Our Goal with this Code of Conduct 

The goal of this document is to set the overall tone for our community of learning. Our classroom spaces and tools should be a safe space for learning, not a place to push the boundaries of good taste. This isn’t an exhaustive list of things you can and can't do. Please take this document in the spirit in which it’s intended and try to be your best self. 

Reporting Harassment or Inappropriate Behaviour 

If you are being harassed while using any of the online tools required by your Red River College classes, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns, please contact your course instructor or the department coordinator. We will take all good-faith reports of harassment seriously.
