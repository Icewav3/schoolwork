Communicating with your Instructor and Peers

There are a variety of ways for you to communicate with your instructor and peers in the online learning environment. LEARN includes a number of tools available for you to engage with your instructor and other students enrolled in the course on a regular basis, as well as ask specific course-related questions.

Communication Tools

News

Your instructor will post frequent news items to the course homepage. News items will help keep you on task and offer important announcements about the course. 

Teams

You have been added to a MS Team dedicated to this course. The majority of your course lectures will take place in channel within this Team. The quickest way to contact your instructor will be through a direct message (DM) in MS Teams.

Email

You can use email to communicate with your course instructor and other students in the course. Send an email to your instructor or peers by selecting their name in the Classlist, available from the NavBar. Allow up to 48 hours for your instructor to respond to course-related inquiries.
